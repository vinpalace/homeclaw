Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

homeclaw is a private home automation project used only by the members of one household. It isn't offered to the public.

Google Calendar data

With a household member's permission, homeclaw reads their Google Calendar to check when they are free, and creates, changes and deletes workout events.

How data is stored and processed

  • Sign-in tokens and workout plans are stored on a computer in the household's home, and in the household's own private backups.
  • When a household member asks for help, relevant calendar details are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service to process the request.
  • Calendar data isn't sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Removing access

A household member can remove homeclaw's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the project's GitHub page.
